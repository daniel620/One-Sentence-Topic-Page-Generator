Bulgaria Wins Eurovision 2026 in Vienna with "Bangaranga"

Dara's victory marks Bulgaria's first-ever win at the 70th edition of the contest, held May 16 in Austria's capital.

Bulgaria's Dara won the Eurovision Song Contest 2026 with "Bangaranga" at the Grand Final on May 16 in Vienna, claiming the country's first victory in the competition's 70-year history. The 70th edition saw 35 countries compete, including returnees Moldova, Bulgaria, and Romania after multi-year absences. With a record 25 languages featured across entries, the contest concluded after two semi-finals and the final at Vienna's Wiener Stadthalle.
