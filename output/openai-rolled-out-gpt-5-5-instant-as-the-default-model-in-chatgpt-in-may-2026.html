OpenAI Makes GPT-5.5 Instant the Default ChatGPT Model for All Users

The upgraded model brings improved photo analysis, STEM capabilities, and smarter web search to hundreds of millions.

On May 5, 2026, OpenAI announced GPT-5.5 Instant as the new default model for ChatGPT, available to all users including free tier. The model builds on GPT-5.5, released April 23, 2026, and delivers improvements in analyzing photos, answering STEM questions, and deciding when to use web search. Unlike premium models like Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 Pro, GPT-5.5 Instant is accessible to everyone at no cost.
