OpenAI Replaces ChatGPT Default with GPT-5.5 Instant

The new model rolled out to free users May 5–6, replacing GPT-5.3 Instant across the platform.

OpenAI upgraded ChatGPT's default model to GPT-5.5 Instant on May 5–6, 2026, replacing GPT-5.3 Instant for free-tier users. The company announced the model emphasizes smarter conversational understanding, clearer responses, and personalization improvements, along with optimization for agentic AI and real-world work tasks. Paid-tier rollout timelines remain unconfirmed.
