UAE Exits OPEC After Six Decades, Citing Iran Attacks and Export Constraints

The cartel loses its third-largest producer amid geopolitical turmoil and elevated oil prices.

The United Arab Emirates officially withdrew from OPEC on May 1, 2026, ending nearly 60 years of membership that began in 1967. The departure follows weeks of missile and drone attacks by fellow OPEC member Iran and shipping disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz that severely constrained UAE oil exports. As OPEC's third-largest producer, the UAE's exit removes about 15% of the cartel's capacity and comes amid a nine-week US-Israeli conflict with Iran that has pushed oil prices above $110 per barrel.
