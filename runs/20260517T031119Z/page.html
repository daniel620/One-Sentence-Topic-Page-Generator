OpenAI Launches GPT-5.5 Instant as Default ChatGPT Model

The new foundation model replaces GPT-5.3 Instant, arriving less than two months after GPT-5.4 in the fastest upgrade cycle yet.

OpenAI released GPT-5.5 Instant in early May 2026, making it the new default model for ChatGPT users. The launch represents the latest step in an accelerating development cycle, with GPT-5.5 arriving less than two months after its predecessor GPT-5.4. OpenAI President Greg Brockman highlighted the model's ability to accomplish more with less guidance, while the company confirmed GPT-5.5 meets "High" cybersecurity risk criteria but does not cross the "Critical" threshold.
