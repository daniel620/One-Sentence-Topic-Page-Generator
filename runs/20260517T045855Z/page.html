Bulgaria Wins Eurovision Song Contest 2026 in Vienna

The 70th Eurovision Song Contest concluded in Vienna with 25 countries competing in the Grand Final at Wiener Stadthalle.

Bulgaria claimed victory at the Eurovision Song Contest 2026, held May 12-16 in Vienna, Austria. The 70th edition of the competition brought 25 songs to the Grand Final stage at the Wiener Stadthalle on May 16. Austria served as host after winning the 2025 contest, with broadcaster ORF producing the event for a venue capacity of up to 16,000 attendees.
