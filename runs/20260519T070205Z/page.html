Bulgaria Wins Eurovision 2026 with DARA's "Bangaranga"

DARA delivered Bulgaria's first-ever Eurovision victory in Vienna, earning 516 points in the 70th contest.

Bulgaria won the Eurovision Song Contest 2026 on May 16 in Vienna, with DARA's "Bangaranga" earning 516 points and securing the nation's first-ever victory in the competition's 70-year history. The contest featured 35 participating broadcasters and introduced new rules including the return of jury voting to semi-finals at 50% weighting.
