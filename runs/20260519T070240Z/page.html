OpenAI Releases GPT-5.5 Instant as New Default ChatGPT Model

The faster, smarter model replaces GPT-5.3 Instant and brings improved intelligence while matching GPT-5.4 latency.

OpenAI launched GPT-5.5 Instant in early May 2026, making it the default model for all logged-in ChatGPT users. The new model delivers GPT-5.5-level intelligence at GPT-5.4 speed, with a 1,050,000-token context window and knowledge through December 2025. Free users get 10 messages every 5 hours, while the full GPT-5.5 and Pro variants remain available to paid subscribers.
