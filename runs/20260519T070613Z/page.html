2026 FIFA World Cup Opens June 11 at Estadio Azteca

The first 48-team World Cup kicks off in Mexico City, spanning three host nations across North America.

The 2026 FIFA World Cup will open on June 11, 2026, at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City with Mexico facing South Africa at 8pm UK time. This 23rd edition marks a historic expansion to 48 teams and will be the first World Cup co-hosted by three nations: the United States, Canada, and Mexico. The tournament will feature 103 matches across 16 venues over 37 days, with 32 teams advancing to the knockout rounds.
